## Task 02: Verify cleanup

### Introduction

After deletion completes, confirm that all resources and associated services are removed from your subscription.

### Description

You’ll verify that no Sentinel workspaces, storage accounts, or virtual machines remain active.

### Success criteria

- No active Sentinel workspaces or virtual machines remain in the portal.  
- Azure costs for the lab are stopped.

---

1. In the Azure portal search bar, type **Microsoft Sentinel** and verify no active workspaces are listed.

1. Search for **Virtual machines** and confirm that `vm-sentinel-lab` no longer exists.

1. Search for **Storage accounts** and confirm that `sentinelstor@lab.LabInstance.Id` no longer appears.

1. Search for **Log Analytics workspaces** to ensure that `law-sentinel-xdr-lab` and `law-custom-sentinel-lab` (if created) are removed.

{: .highlight }
> If any resources remain, verify that they belong to a different resource group or subscription before deleting them manually.
